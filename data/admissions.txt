# Admission process
B.Tech admissions are made through the state entrance counselling process (convener quota) and through the management quota. Candidates must register for counselling, choose web options and report to the college with their allotment letter.

# Eligibility
Candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, and hold a valid rank in the state engineering entrance examination.

# Required documents
- Allotment letter and rank card
- 10th and Intermediate marks memos
- Transfer certificate and study certificates
- Caste and income certificates, where applicable
- Passport-size photographs
- Aadhaar card

# Important dates
Admission dates follow the official state counselling schedule and are announced each year.
