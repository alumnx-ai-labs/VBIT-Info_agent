# Undergraduate programmes (B.Tech)
VBIT offers four-year B.Tech programmes in the following branches:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)

# Intake
Sample intake per branch: CSE 180 seats, CSE (AI and ML) 60 seats, ECE 120 seats, EEE 60 seats.

# Curriculum
Each B.Tech programme has eight semesters covering core subjects, electives, laboratories, a mini project and a final-year major project.

# Postgraduate programmes
M.Tech and MBA programmes are listed as sample entries only: M.Tech (CSE) and MBA.
